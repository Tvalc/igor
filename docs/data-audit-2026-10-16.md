## Corrections worth knowing

- **Makko AI**: commercial use is allowed on every plan; creators keep full ownership and Makko takes no revenue share (Makko pricing-page FAQ).
- **Gambo AI**: shut down on 1 May 2026; now listed as discontinued with Rosebud AI as the replacement.
- **Rosebud AI**: commercial rights only on the 10x Dev plan ($30/mo) and above, not the $15 plan.
- **Kaedim**: the cheapest plan is $400/mo; the $50 previously listed was a 7-day trial.
- **Unity AI**: Unity Learn says the open beta is for non-commercial and testing use until general release (may be dated; re-check).
- **Sprite-AI**: has a free tier (15 generations); paid plans from $8/mo.
- **Bitmagic**: now has a free web Creator and a paid Pro plan; description updated.
- **Godot AI**: about 41–46 MCP tools, not "80+"; tagline corrected.
- **Luma Genie**: the $30/mo price came from a third-party site and was removed.
- **ElevenLabs / NVIDIA ACE**: engine plugins we could not confirm on vendor pages were removed (ElevenLabs: Unity only; ACE: Unreal only).
- **Cursor**: now sold inside xAI's subscription; cheapest plan $8/mo (Grok models only).
- **Soundraw**: licence page says unedited tracks stay licensed only while subscribed, which matters for shipped games.
- **Meshy**: free-plan models are CC BY 4.0 (credit required); free users may not be able to download them.
